Earth texture attribution

The Earth day, night-lights, cloud, normal, and specular maps in this folder are
from Solar System Scope's texture pack:
https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/

Solar System Scope distributes these textures under the Creative Commons
Attribution 4.0 International license:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

The texture pack documentation states that its Earth maps incorporate NASA
elevation and imagery data, including NASA's Blue Marble.
